Race, gender and voting method

These views include early and absentee ballots only. They do not include Election Day demographics. The percentage shown is a share of these ballots, not the percentage of everyone in that group who voted.

Official early and absentee reports

Sources, dates and calculation details

Parish returns were checked November 4 at 8:28 p.m. Central. The early and absentee report was printed November 4 at 3:10 a.m. Central. The full election demographic report was not available in that review. Registration uses the November 1 report.

SOS calls the gender columns sex and reports male and female. Counts that do not fit those two totals are left unclassified. These records do not reveal how any person voted.
