How the November 3 House races work

All candidates in each U.S. House contest appear on the same ballot regardless of party. A candidate needs more than half the votes cast in that race to win outright. If no candidate receives a majority, a December 12 election follows, ordinarily between the two leading candidates. Tie rules can affect who advances.

07 / SOURCES AND ACCOUNTABILITY

Every number has a source

Official source files

Louisiana Secretary of State early voting workbooks downloaded November 4 cover the November election through November 3. Both workbooks and their checksums are preserved. Their print footer is November 4, 2026, 3:10:08 a.m. Central Time.

SOS early voting statistics ↗

Reconciliation

All 64 parish totals and 6,314 detailed rows reconcile across both workbooks. Voting method, race and party totals match. Reported male and female counts leave a residual of 278, which is not assigned a gender.

Methods and validation record

Geographic limits

January 2026 Census parish polygons are matched one to one using a checked name crosswalk and Census GEOIDs. SOS parish codes are not Census FIPS codes. Parish results are not assigned to congressional districts.

Boundary provenance

October 27 and November 3 participation snapshots are preserved separately. Snapshot differences are administrative revisions, not a daily voting series. The parish map shows early and absentee counts or shares, not full election turnout.
