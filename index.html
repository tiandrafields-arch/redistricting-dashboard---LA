05 / SOURCES & ACCOUNTABILITY

Every number has a source

Official source files

Louisiana Secretary of State statewide and parish workbooks supplied for this project. Both cover the November 3 election through October 27. Their print footers are displayed as October 28, 2026, 3:10:06 a.m. Central Time, using the publisher’s confirmed Louisiana reporting timezone.

SOS early voting statistics ↗

Reconciliation

All 64 parish totals and 6,869 detailed report rows reconcile across the two workbooks. Voting method, race and party totals match. Reported male and female counts leave a residual of 266, which is not assigned a gender.

Methods and validation record

Geographic limits

January 2026 Census parish polygons are matched one to one using a checked name crosswalk and Census GEOIDs. SOS parish codes are not Census FIPS codes. Parish results are not assigned to congressional districts.

Boundary provenance

Only one reporting cutoff is authenticated in these uploads. No daily growth series or prior cycle comparison is invented. The map shows counts or shares of recorded ballots, never a turnout rate without an appropriate registration denominator.
