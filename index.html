SOURCES AND ACCOUNTABILITY

Know what each view measures

Overview

Parish comparisons use the same statewide Senate contest. Unofficial turnout equals votes recorded in that contest divided by its qualified voter count, rounded to one decimal place using the SOS method. It is not an audited count of everyone who voted in any race.

Official SOS results · Preserved parish returns

Race, gender and method

These views cover early and absentee participation through November 3. SOS has not supplied the audited full election demographic participation for this release. Zero placeholders in unavailable participation fields are not displayed as zero turnout. SOS calls its gender columns sex. Any unallocated difference remains unclassified.
