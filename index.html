What changed from the old dashboard?

The former May whole-parish approximation estimated that 81% of Black registrants were outside District 2. The official October congressional subtotals show 632,478 of 933,052 Black registrants — 67.79% — outside District 2. District 2 now shows 300,574 Black registrants, or 60.08% of district registration. The 81% finding is withdrawn.

Turnout forecast: not published yet

A turnout prediction should not be generated from registration alone. The next phase requires official historical election results, a reproducible geographic crosswalk to the 2026 map, comparable-election definitions, back-testing, error metrics, and uncertainty intervals.
