# Changelog

## 2026-10-21 — Voter-tool pivot and October registration refresh

### Changed

- Archived the prior redistricting-focused public dashboard.
- Refocused the live site toward current congressional voter information and participation data.
- Replaced the May 1, 2026 whole-parish congressional registration approximation with Louisiana Secretary of State October 1, 2026 congressional-district subtotal rows.
- Updated statewide registration totals to 2,978,143 registered voters.
- Updated statewide Black registration to 933,052 (31.33%).
- Updated statewide party registration to 1,060,252 Democratic, 1,067,827 Republican, 825,562 No Party, and 24,502 other-party registrants.
- Replaced the former 81% Black-registrants-outside-District-2 estimate with 67.79% (632,478 of 933,052).
- Updated District 2 Black registration from 175,996 (51.4%) to 300,574 (60.08%).
- Added official Louisiana voter-resource links and current November 3 election timing.
- Removed registration language that implied turnout, preferred-candidate performance, or population malapportionment.

### Unchanged / archived

- Census 2020 demographic and VAP analysis remains part of the archived redistricting work.
- VAP-based REI is not used as a turnout measure.
- Historical legal/redistricting narrative is preserved in the archive rather than presented as the live site's primary function.

### Turnout forecasting

No turnout forecast is published in this release. Forecasting is reserved for the Louisiana Congressional Representation & Participation Lab after historical election data, geography crosswalks, back-testing, and uncertainty reporting are complete.
