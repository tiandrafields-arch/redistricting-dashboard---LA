## Method

The statewide workbook supplies the party-and-race control totals. The parish workbook contains congressional-district subtotal rows within parish sheets. Those rows were aggregated by congressional district.

QA results:

- 79 congressional subtotal rows;
- 64 parishes represented;
- 15 split parishes represented;
- six congressional district aggregates;
- zero difference from statewide controls for total registration and every reported party-and-race field.

The October district subtotals replace the prior whole-parish majority assignment. The May parish-level table is preserved only in the legacy archive and should not be treated as the current district-registration source.

## Definitions and limitations

These data describe registered voters. They do not measure:

- Census voting-age population;
- citizen voting-age population;
- ballots cast or turnout;
- candidate preference;
- racial bloc voting;
- election outcomes;
- legal liability.
